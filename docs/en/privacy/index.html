Privacy Policy

Published: June 8, 2026 · Website: defindings.com

deFindings is a non-commercial academic media project: the graduation work of Julie Arkhipova at HSE Art&Design School (2026). The website does not sell goods or services, does not display advertising, and does not use personal data for commercial purposes.

This policy describes what data is collected within this project, why it is needed, and what rights website visitors have. The document has been prepared in accordance with the requirements of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data."

1. Personal Data Controller

The personal data controller within the deFindings academic project is Julie Arkhipova — the author, editor, and graduate of HSE Art&Design School. The project is created and published for academic and research purposes as part of the 2026 graduation work.

Educational institution: HSE University (National Research University Higher School of Economics), School of Design.

Contact for personal data inquiries: arkhipoovau@gmail.com

2. What Data We Process

The following categories of data may be processed on the website:

  • Email address — if you subscribe to the deFindings newsletter.
  • Technical and anonymized data — IP address, browser and device type, language, pages you visit, referral source, date and time of visit, cookies, and similar identifiers.
  • Data from correspondence — if you write to us by email (sender address and message content).

We do not collect special categories of personal data (health, political views, etc.) and do not ask you to provide them.

3. Purposes of Processing

Data is processed only to the extent necessary for the academic project:

  • informing subscribers about new deFindings content (voluntary newsletter);
  • responding to project-related inquiries;
  • ensuring website operation and security;
  • tracking visitor statistics as part of audience analysis for the academic media project.

Data is not used for targeted advertising, is not shared as lists with third parties, and is not sold.

4. Legal Basis

We process personal data on the following grounds:

  • Consent — when subscribing to the newsletter, you voluntarily confirm your consent to the processing of your email address by clicking the submit button.
  • Fulfillment of your request — when you contact us by email regarding the project.
  • Minimally necessary analytics — to understand how visitors use the academic website, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The website uses cookies and similar technologies for web analytics services. On your first visit, a notice appears at the bottom of the screen: analytics (Yandex.Metrica and Google Analytics) are enabled only after you click "Accept." Until then, no counters are loaded. Your choice is saved in your browser.

The website uses:

  • Yandex.Metrica (Yandex LLC, Russia) — counter No. 109727534. Yandex Policy
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, USA) — ID G-45ZC96BSF5. Google Policy
  • MailerLite — when subscribing to the newsletter and opening emails. MailerLite Policy

You can restrict cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the correct operation of certain website features.

6. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We share data only with those services that are necessary for the website and newsletter:

  • MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, EU) — subscriber list storage and email delivery;
  • GitHub Pages (Microsoft/GitHub, USA) — static website hosting;
  • Yandex.Metrica and Google Analytics — visitor statistics.

Some data may be processed outside the Russian Federation. Transfer is carried out in compliance with Federal Law 152-FZ, including with your consent where required.

7. Data Retention

  • Subscriber email — until you unsubscribe, until your request for deletion, or until the deFindings academic project ends, unless otherwise required by law.
  • Analytics data — for the periods set by Yandex.Metrica and Google Analytics (according to counter settings).
  • Email correspondence — for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain the project, unless you request earlier deletion.

8. Your Rights

In accordance with Federal Law 152-FZ, you have the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of your personal data;
  • request correction, blocking, or deletion of your data;
  • withdraw your consent to processing (including unsubscribing from the newsletter);
  • file a complaint with Roskomnadzor.

To exercise your rights, write to arkhipoovau@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days.

You can also unsubscribe from the newsletter using the "Unsubscribe" link in any deFindings email.

9. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect data within the academic project: we use HTTPS, restrict access to newsletter and analytics services, do not publish subscriber emails on the website, and do not share them for commercial use.

10. Academic Nature of the Project

deFindings is published as a graduation and research project. After the academic phase ends, the newsletter may be discontinued, and accumulated subscriber data may be deleted or anonymized, unless otherwise agreed with subscribers or required by law.

By continuing to use the website and subscribing to the newsletter, you acknowledge the non-commercial and academic nature of the project.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page. In case of significant changes, the publication date will be updated at the top of the document.
