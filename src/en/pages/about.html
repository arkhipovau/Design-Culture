The project was conceived and built by Julie Arkhipova, a graduate of HSE Art&Design School.

deFindings is designed as a creative room for conversation. These are journals, posters, notes, and artifacts that surround a designer every day — only now digitized. One editor assembles each issue by hand and publishes it when the material is ready to be shown. Careful work matters here, but mistakes are allowed.
